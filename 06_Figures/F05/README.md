# F05 · Biology of the sex difference

- **a** Pathways whose phenotype link differs by sex (phenotype fgsea, female fit and male minus
  female slope): OXPHOS and aerobic respiration against soleus pyruvate conductance, ECM, EMT and
  myogenesis.
- **b** WGCNA modules: top term, PAS contrasts, within-group phenotype slopes. One link at
  p ≤ 0.05 (brown with CSA); no module differs by sex × treatment.
- **c** OXPHOS against soleus pyruvate conductance: pooled NES −2.39 (FDR 5×10⁻¹⁰); slope
  difference NES −2.08 (FDR 2×10⁻⁶).
- **d** Leading-edge proteins of the strongest interaction terms: contractile proteins (Tnnc2,
  Myl1, Mybph, Tpm1, Acta1) rise with PAS in females and fall in males.
