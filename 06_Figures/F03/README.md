# F03 · Do the sexes respond differently?

Only the treatment × sex contrast says the sexes differ; a hit in one sex alone is "detected in"
that sex.

- **a** Female vs male PAS effect per protein, coloured by sex-pattern label.
- **b** Features at p ≤ 0.05 per label: 318 proteins (156 sex-differential, 7 shared, 75 detected
  in females, 80 in males); no WGCNA module.
- **c** Signature carry-over (limma::fry): neither sex's PAS proteins move the same way in the
  other sex (all p > 0.5).
- **d** Interaction rings: myogenesis, EMT, muscle contraction and ECM rise more in females (7 at
  FDR ≤ 0.05); mitochondrial carbohydrate and lipid metabolism up, central dogma down (3).

Sources: `05_Summary/04_Sex_Patterns`, `05_Summary/07_Signature`, `03_Pathway_Enrichment/08_Display`.
