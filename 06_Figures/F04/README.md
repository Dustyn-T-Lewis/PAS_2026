# F04 · Signature and phenotype

Phenotypes come from soleus, plantaris and IHC; the proteome from gastrocnemius. Associations only.

- **a** PAS minus vehicle per phenotype and sex: in females capillary density +1.7 SD (interaction
  p = 0.014), CSA +0.7 SD, satellite cells +1.3 SD; conductances unchanged.
- **b** Proteins (FDR ≤ 0.10) and pathways (FDR ≤ 0.05) per phenotype: CSA leads for proteins
  (327 pooled); soleus pyruvate conductance for pathways (205 female, 271 slope difference).
- **c** Female signature score against the three changed phenotypes: groups separate, but within
  groups the score does not track the trait (all slope p > 0.1).
- **d** Classification, balanced error rate: females reach 0.10 with phenotypes, the combined
  proteome and both (permutation p ≤ 0.05); males only with modules.
