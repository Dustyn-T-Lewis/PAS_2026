# 01_Fit

Every model the comparisons read, fitted once on all 20 mice.

| | |
|---|---|
| Reads | `01_Processing/02_Quantification/c_data/proteins.rds`, `01_Processing/03_Design/c_data/design.rds`, `01_Processing/05_Scores/c_data/scores.rds`, `01_Processing/06_Modules/c_data/modules.rds`, `01_Processing/04_Gene_Sets/c_data/gene_sets.rds`, `00_Setup/Input/phenotypes.xlsx` |
| Writes | `c_data/01_fit.xlsx`, `c_data/fit.rds` (read by 02_Enrichment, 03_Classification, 04_PCA, 06_Candidates, 07_Sensitivity, `03_Comparisons` steps 01, 03 and 04, and `04_Figures/` F01, F02, F05, S2, S3 and S7), `b_reports/01_fit.pdf` |
| Run | `Rscript 02_Statistics/01_Fit/a_script/01_fit.R` |

- **Proteins:** `limpa::dpcDE(sample.weights = TRUE)` per universe on the design's four group means and surrogate variable. Each contrast is refitted as the first coefficient of an equivalent design (`limma::contrastAsCoef()`) and moderated with `eBayes()`; BH within universe and contrast; Π = p^|log2 FC| beside p and FDR; r with group (first group = 1) beside each two-group contrast.
- **PAS in either sex:** a moderated F over the two PAS contrasts, both made the first coefficients of an equivalent design so the test stays exact; it covers the interaction, their difference. Each protein gets a response pattern from the signs of its two fold changes. Result: 215 full-proteome proteins at F p ≤ 0.05, none at FDR ≤ 0.05: 83 down in both sexes, 56 up in both, 56 up in females and down in males, 20 down in females and up in males (`protein_ftest`).
- **Scores and eigengenes:** plain limma on the same design and contrasts.
- **Shown score sets:** `members` counts each set's measured members, its genes among the universe's proteins, as 05_Scores ranks them. Within each contrast and universe, the pathways, complexes and clusters at singscore p ≤ 0.05 are grouped by `representative_sets()` (`helpers.R`) on those members, as 02_Enrichment groups fgsea sets: Jaccard tree cut at 0.70, each group shown by its most specific set within 10-fold of its best p. `overlap_group` and `shown` carry the result in `score_contrasts`; modules share no members and stay NA. In the three comparison contrasts this shows 42 of 98 sets at p ≤ 0.05 in females, 26 of 55 in males and 41 of 70 for the interaction; F05 draws the shown CORUM and MitoCarta sets.
- **Phenotype associations:** each feature regressed on one z-scored phenotype within one sex, the surrogate variable fitted within each sex too, under two models. `pooled` (`~0 + sex + z:sex + sv1:sex`) asks whether a feature co-varies with the phenotype across PAS and vehicle mice; `treatment` (`~0 + group + z:sex + sv1:sex`) asks whether it tracks the phenotype among mice given the same treatment. Both are co-movement, not a PAS effect; r pooled and within each group sits beside them. A pooled refit with the pellet's mitochondrial share (centred within group) is kept as a check (`pooled_mito`).
- **Phenotype effects:** PAS against the same sex's vehicle as % change (Welch t on log values); the interaction from `lm(log(value) ~ treatment * sex)`, as the female response relative to the male.
- **Result (full proteome, 1,962 tests):** female 177 at p ≤ 0.05, 107 at Π ≤ 0.05, 3 at FDR ≤ 0.05 (Cops3, Cops8 and Dusp3, all down); male 193, 106, 0; interaction 162, 142, 0. Phenotype effects in females: capillary density +138% (p 0.005), CSA +22% (p 0.002), satellite cells +47% (p 0.009); none in males; interaction only for capillary density (p 0.014).
- **Module eigengenes:** ordinary least-squares t on the residual df, no `eBayes()`: the prior is estimated from the spread of variances across features, and eight standardised eigengenes cannot inform it (moderation set their df to about 120).
- **Effect size on one scale:** every limma table also carries `r_equiv = t / sqrt(t^2 + df)`, the r-equivalent of the t on the df it is tested against (residual plus prior df for moderated tables), so a contrast and a phenotype association can share one colour scale in the figures; unlike Pearson r it comes from the same test as the p beside it.
- **Workbook:** `protein_contrasts` and `score_contrasts` hold every contrast, the score rows with `members`, `overlap_group` and `shown`; `protein_associations` and `score_associations` every phenotype association; `association_summary` the counts by `model`.
- **Basis:** limpa vignette, "Differential expression" (DE must go through `dpcDE`; `sample.weights = TRUE` downweights outlying samples); limma's help for `contrasts.fit()` and `contrastAsCoef()`: contrasts.fit takes coefficient correlations as common to all features, exact only for orthogonal designs or equal weights, which limpa's per-protein weights and the surrogate variable rule out; Smyth 2004 for `eBayes()`; limma User's Guide on covariates for the treatment-adjusted association. Recomputed by hand, logFC and residual SD match limma to 1e-13; t matches limma's legacy moderation to 1e-13, and the saved t, from limma's current prior for unequal residual df, differs from that by at most 0.06.
- **Note:** The script stops if an r fails to join its row, or if a contrast row would take more than one r.
