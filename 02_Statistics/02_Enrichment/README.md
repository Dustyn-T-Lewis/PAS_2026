- **Contrasts:** `enrichVolcano::run_enrichment(tests = "fgsea")` on the moderated t of each comparison's contrast, one contrast at a time, seed 20260910. GO:BP, Reactome, Hallmark and KEGG LEGACY in the full proteome, MitoCarta in the mitochondrial universe; BH within contrast and collection. Each row carries the same set's singscore contrast from `02_Statistics/01_Fit` (log2 FC, p, FDR, r with group), which is tested across mice, and its camera p and FDR: `limma::camera` on the same design and contrast, unweighted, with the set's inter-protein correlation estimated. camera is reported in workbooks only; no figure draws it.
- **Redundancy:** `enrichVolcano::dedup_terms(method = "collapse_pathways")`, which runs `fgsea::collapsePathways` within each contrast and collection: an FDR-level term is `redundant` when it is no longer enriched once a stronger term's genes are conditioned on, and `kept` otherwise; `merged_into` names its parent. Chosen over EnrichmentMap, set cover and affinity propagation in a trial; nothing is dropped, and 06_Candidates reads the flag for its pathway route. The phenotype-association fgsea carries the same flag, run within each model, sex, universe and phenotype on that phenotype's protein ranking.
- **Shown sets:** within each test (a contrast and universe, or a model, sex and phenotype over both universes), the sets at FDR ≤ 0.05 are grouped by `representative_sets()` (`helpers.R`): average-linkage clustering on the Jaccard distance between tested members, cut at 0.70, with each group shown by its most specific set within 10-fold of the group's best p. `overlap_group` and `shown` carry the result in `results` and `association_fgsea`; F03 and F04 draw the shown sets. `cluster_ora` carries no grouping; S2 draws each cluster's three sets with the smallest p among those at p ≤ 0.05.
- **Other tests:** GO Slim compartments; the literature and ROS sets as one list, BH across it; fgsea on each protein's phenotype-association t per sex and association model (co-movement, not a PAS effect), beside the singscore association and camera on the same slope; cross-sex signatures (each sex's Π ≤ 0.05 up and down proteins tested in the other sex's PAS contrast by `limma::fry`, across mice, unweighted and with `standardize = "none"`, on the fit's design reparametrised by `contrastAsCoef()` so that contrast is the first coefficient); ORA (`fgsea::fora`) against every detected gene, on the full-proteome collections with BH within list and collection, of each response pattern of the PAS F-test proteins (`pattern_ora`) and of each co-expression cluster in the S2 heatmaps (`cluster_ora`, BH within cluster and collection); STRING v12 connectivity of the Π and FDR protein lists.
- **S2 clusters:** each comparison contrast's Π ≤ 0.05 proteins (full proteome), split by direction, are clustered within each direction on the values S2 draws, log2 abundance minus the own-sex vehicle mean with undetected cells missing (`proteins.rds` observations): average-linkage on 1 − Pearson r over the mice both proteins were detected in, r = 0 for a pair sharing fewer than 5 (`coexpression_tree()` in `helpers.R`, the tree S6 a orders by). `dynamicTreeCut::cutreeDynamic(method = "hybrid", deepSplit = 2, minClusterSize = 10)` cuts each tree (Langfelder et al. 2008). `heatmap_clusters` gives every protein's row in S2's drawing order, its direction and its cluster, numbered C1, C2, … down each panel; the 13 proteins the cut leaves unassigned (3 female, 7 male, 3 interaction) sit at their block's end with no cluster and no ORA. Female: up C1 (43), down C2 (51) and C3 (10); male: up C1 (12) and C2 (15), down C3 (26) and C4 (46); treatment × sex: F > M C1 (14), C2 (30) and C3 (50), F < M C4 (24) and C5 (21).
- **Result (p ≤ 0.05 / FDR ≤ 0.05):** female GO:BP 95 / 10, Reactome 24 / 0, Hallmark 2 / 1, KEGG 6 / 0, MitoCarta 11 / 2; male GO:BP 161 / 35, Reactome 16 / 0, Hallmark 6 / 1, KEGG 7 / 5, MitoCarta 6 / 0; treatment × sex GO:BP 93 / 20, Reactome 19 / 6, Hallmark 3 / 3, KEGG 8 / 2, MitoCarta 12 / 11. collapsePathways keeps 8 of 13 FDR-level terms in females, 12 of 41 in males and 22 of 42 for the interaction; the overlap rule shows 8, 14 and 24. No cross-sex signature reached p ≤ 0.05 in the other sex by fry (p 0.074 to 0.98), although 50 to 78% of each list moves the same way. Pattern ORA: proteins up in females and down in males are enriched for muscle contraction (Reactome striated muscle contraction FDR 6e-6, muscle contraction 0.001; Hallmark myogenesis 0.03); no other pattern has a term at FDR ≤ 0.05. Cluster ORA: sets reach FDR ≤ 0.05 in four clusters, male C3 (24 sets; Reactome striated muscle contraction FDR 5e-7), male C4 (5; GO:BP sensory system development 0.007), interaction C3 (36; Hallmark myogenesis 6e-9) and interaction C2 (2; Hallmark EMT 0.002); no female cluster has one (smallest FDR 0.17, C2, Reactome cell cycle). The clusters come from proteins chosen for responding and hold 10 to 51 proteins, so this ORA describes them and tests little.
- **Basis:** fgsea (Korotkevich et al.) through enrichVolcano for the main tests; the compartment and literature lists call `fgsea::fgsea(nPermSimple = 10000)` directly, as fgsea advises when a set's gene-level statistics are unbalanced, because enrichVolcano keeps the default and such a set would lose its p-value; Korotkevich et al. for `collapsePathways`; Wu & Smyth 2012 for fry, and on why gene-permutation tests run optimistic when genes are correlated, which is why the cross-sex signatures moved from fgsea to fry: fgsea had put three of them at p ≤ 0.006. NES rebuilt from the GSEA definition with 20,000 random sets matches within 0.035.
- **Note:** The fgsea test permutes genes, so its FDR runs optimistic against the singscore test; several FDR-level MitoCarta terms have singscore p > 0.2. The STRING lists are descriptive: subunits of one complex vary together.
