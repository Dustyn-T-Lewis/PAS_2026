# 02_Enrichment

Runs every pathway test once, so the comparisons only slice the results.

|---|---|
| Reads | `01_Processing/04_Gene_Sets/c_data/gene_sets.rds`, `01_Processing/03_Design/c_data/design.rds`, `02_Statistics/01_Fit/c_data/fit.rds`, `00_Setup/Input/STRING_v12/` |
| Writes | `c_data/02_enrichment.xlsx`, `c_data/enrichment.rds` (read by 06_Candidates, `03_Comparisons` steps 01 to 03, and `04_Figures/` F01, F03, F04, S2, S3 and S6), `b_reports/02_enrichment.pdf` |
| Run | `Rscript 02_Statistics/02_Enrichment/a_script/02_enrichment.R` |

- **Contrasts:** `enrichVolcano::run_enrichment(tests = "fgsea")` on the moderated t of each comparison's contrast, one contrast at a time, seed 20260910. GO:BP, Reactome, Hallmark and KEGG LEGACY in the full proteome, MitoCarta in the mitochondrial universe; BH within contrast and collection. Each row carries the same set's singscore contrast from `02_Statistics/01_Fit` (log2 FC, p, FDR, r with group), which is tested across mice, and its camera p and FDR: `limma::camera` on the same design and contrast, unweighted, with the set's inter-protein correlation estimated. camera is reported in workbooks only; no figure draws it.
- **Redundancy:** `enrichVolcano::dedup_terms(method = "collapse_pathways")`, which runs `fgsea::collapsePathways` within each contrast and collection: an FDR-level term is `redundant` when it is no longer enriched once a stronger term's genes are conditioned on, and `kept` otherwise; `merged_into` names its parent. Chosen over EnrichmentMap, set cover and affinity propagation in a trial; nothing is dropped, and 06_Candidates reads the flag for its pathway route. The phenotype-association fgsea carries the same flag, run within each model, sex, universe and phenotype on that phenotype's protein ranking.
- **Shown sets:** within each test (a contrast and universe, or a model, sex and phenotype over both universes), the sets at FDR ≤ 0.05 are grouped by `representative_sets()` (`helpers.R`): average-linkage clustering on the Jaccard distance between tested members, cut at 0.70, with each group shown by its most specific set within 10-fold of the group's best p. `overlap_group` and `shown` carry the result in `results` and `association_fgsea`; F03 and F04 draw the shown sets. `block_ora` carries no grouping; S2 draws each list's five sets with the smallest p among those at p ≤ 0.05.
- **Other tests:** GO Slim compartments; the literature and ROS sets as one list, BH across it; fgsea on each protein's phenotype-association t per sex and association model (co-movement, not a PAS effect), beside the singscore association and camera on the same slope; cross-sex signatures (each sex's Π ≤ 0.05 up and down proteins tested in the other sex's PAS contrast by `limma::fry`, across mice, unweighted and with `standardize = "none"`, on the fit's design reparametrised by `contrastAsCoef()` so that contrast is the first coefficient); ORA (`fgsea::fora`) against every detected gene, on the full-proteome collections with BH within list and collection, of each response pattern of the PAS F-test proteins (`pattern_ora`) and of each comparison contrast's Π ≤ 0.05 proteins split by direction, the S2 heatmap blocks (`block_ora`); STRING v12 connectivity of the Π and FDR protein lists.
- **Result (p ≤ 0.05 / FDR ≤ 0.05):** female GO:BP 95 / 10, Reactome 24 / 0, Hallmark 2 / 1, KEGG 6 / 0, MitoCarta 11 / 2; male GO:BP 161 / 35, Reactome 16 / 0, Hallmark 6 / 1, KEGG 7 / 5, MitoCarta 6 / 0; treatment × sex GO:BP 93 / 20, Reactome 19 / 6, Hallmark 3 / 3, KEGG 8 / 2, MitoCarta 12 / 11. collapsePathways keeps 8 of 13 FDR-level terms in females, 12 of 41 in males and 22 of 42 for the interaction; the overlap rule shows 8, 14 and 24. No cross-sex signature reached p ≤ 0.05 in the other sex by fry (p 0.074 to 0.98), although 50 to 78% of each list moves the same way. Pattern ORA: proteins up in females and down in males are enriched for muscle contraction (Reactome striated muscle contraction FDR 6e-6, muscle contraction 0.001; Hallmark myogenesis 0.03); no other pattern has a term at FDR ≤ 0.05. Block ORA: 23 sets reach FDR ≤ 0.05 for the male down proteins (Reactome striated muscle contraction FDR 4e-6, Hallmark myogenesis 2e-4, GO:BP supramolecular fiber organization 7e-4) and 33 for the treatment × sex F > M proteins (striated muscle contraction 2e-6, myogenesis 5e-6, Hallmark EMT 4e-5); the female up and down, male up and F < M lists have none (smallest FDR 0.088, female down, Reactome cell cycle).
- **Basis:** fgsea (Korotkevich et al.) through enrichVolcano for the main tests; the compartment and literature lists call `fgsea::fgsea(nPermSimple = 10000)` directly, as fgsea advises when a set's gene-level statistics are unbalanced, because enrichVolcano keeps the default and such a set would lose its p-value; Korotkevich et al. for `collapsePathways`; Wu & Smyth 2012 for fry, and on why gene-permutation tests run optimistic when genes are correlated, which is why the cross-sex signatures moved from fgsea to fry: fgsea had put three of them at p ≤ 0.006. NES rebuilt from the GSEA definition with 20,000 random sets matches within 0.035.
- **Note:** The fgsea test permutes genes, so its FDR runs optimistic against the singscore test; several FDR-level MitoCarta terms have singscore p > 0.2. The STRING lists are descriptive: subunits of one complex vary together.
