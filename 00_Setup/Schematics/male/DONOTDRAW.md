# Do not draw

- icons, pictograms, mice, people, organs, tubes, instruments, logos or decorations
- charts, plots, ROC curves, volcano plots, data points or mock results
- any number that is not written in the spec
- arrows or lines between the step cards
- arrows meaning up- or down-regulation
- the other two comparisons
- a comparison of females and males at baseline
- step names, folder names or file names that are not in the spec
- subscript text
- colour names or colour codes written as text
